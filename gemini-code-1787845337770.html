2. Type 1: Basic Verification

  • Q1: $7848 \div 3,4,9$? Sum $=27 \div 3,9$. Last 2 $=48 \div 4$. [Yes]
  • Q2: $1659 \div 7$? $165 - (9\times2) = 147 \div 7$. [Yes]
  • Q3: $8591 \div 11$? $(9+8)-(5+1) = 11$. [Yes]
  • Q4: Div by 9 & 11? Ans: (b) 10,098 (Sum=18, Diff=0).

3. Type 2: Find Missing Single Digit

  • Q13: $54x32 \div 9 \implies 14+x=18 \implies \mathbf{x=4}$
  • Q14: $734K \div 6 \implies K \in \text{even}$, $14+K \div 3 \implies \mathbf{K=4}$
  • Q15: Largest $x$ in $2365x4 \div 4 \implies x4 \div 4 \implies \mathbf{x=8}$
  • Q16: $396258N \div 8 \implies 58N \div 8 \implies \mathbf{N=4}$
  • Q17: $46393x8 \div 11 \implies (15+x)-18=0 \implies \mathbf{x=3}$
  • Q18: $18x3945 \div 11 \implies 6+x=11 \implies \mathbf{x=5}$
  • Q19: $94x2357 \div 11 \implies 8+x=11 \implies \mathbf{x=3}$

4. Type 3: Two Unknown Digits (x, y)

  • Q20: $3425xy \div 36 (4 \times 9)$:
    • $y=2 \implies 15+x=18 \implies \mathbf{(3,2)}$
    • $y=6 \implies 19+x=27 \implies \mathbf{(8,6)}$
  • Q21: $545884805x6 \div 72 (8 \times 9) \implies 5x6 \div 8 \implies \mathbf{x=3 \text{ or } 7}$.
  • Q22: $789263x84 \div 72 \implies 784/8 (y=4)$, $7+x+4=9 \implies x=2$. $\mathbf{xy=8}$.
  • Q23: $4432A43B \div 9, 5 \implies B=5, A+B+2=9 \implies \mathbf{A+B=7}$.
  • Q24: $1330x558y2 \div 88 \implies 18-(9+x+y)=0 \implies \mathbf{x+y=9}$.

5. Type 4: Smallest/Largest Add or Subtract

  • Q30: Min digit to add to $4531$ for $\div 4$: Next is $32 \implies 32-31=\mathbf{1}$.
  • Q31: Min sub from $210$ for $\div 11$: $210/11 \implies \text{Rem } 1 \implies \mathbf{1}$.
  • Q32: $335 + 5A7 = 8B2 \div 3$: $B=A+4$, Sum $=14+A \implies \mathbf{A=4, B=8}$.
  • Q33: Min digit to add to $81247$ for $\div 3$: Sum $=22$, Next $24 \implies \mathbf{2}$.
  • Q34: Min sub from $6583$ for $\div 5$: Ends in $0 \implies 6583-6580=\mathbf{3}$.
  • Q35: Min digit to add to $5911$ for $\div 9$: Sum $=16$, Next $18 \implies \mathbf{2}$.
